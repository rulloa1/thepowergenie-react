Sound & conference calls

For a Power Genie conference, install VB-CABLE once. Choose CABLE Input here. In the Power Genie room, select CABLE Output as the microphone, unmute, and keep the camera off. Set Windows Output to your speakers or headphones. To join as a separate audio-only guest, use the room invite and choose Ask to join without video.

Chrome may request microphone permission to list devices. The app immediately releases the microphone.
